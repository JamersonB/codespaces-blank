A daily craft built around community

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood gathering place for good bread and familiar faces. Our bakers start early, work in small batches, and make room for both everyday staples and special occasion orders.

Thoughtful ingredients

We aim to source dependable, quality ingredients and work with suppliers who share our care for good food. Ask our team about ingredients before ordering; recipes and supplier availability may change.

The people behind the counter

Our bakers, counter team, and neighbors all help make this a welcoming place. We take pride in answering questions, helping you choose a loaf, and preparing orders for your table.
